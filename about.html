About

Hi, my name is Ben. I'm a double major in Mathematics and Government at Harvard College. For the last two summers I've been a full-stack developer on IBM's security and encryption team, and for three years I've worked for — and then led — Harvard Tech for Social Good, the largest technical organization on campus.

I also like to write. Most recently I was published in the September 1st issue of The Hollywood Reporter, and I'm currently working on a parody novel commissioned by Penguin Random House. On the creative side I wrote this year's Hasty Pudding musical and I'm on staff at The Harvard Lampoon.

Function name or punchline, I care about getting the words right.
