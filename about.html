About

Technically

I'm a double major in Mathematics and Government at Harvard. For the last two summers I've been a full-stack developer on IBM's security and encryption team, and for three years I worked for — and then led — Harvard Tech for Social Good, the largest technical organization on campus.

Otherwise

I also write. Most recently I was published in the September 1 issue of The Hollywood Reporter, and I'm working on a parody novel commissioned by Penguin Random House. I wrote this year's Hasty Pudding musical, and I'm on staff at The Harvard Lampoon.

Function name or punchline, I care about getting the words right.
