About

I recently graduated from Harvard College with a double major in Mathematics and Government, bouncing between courses like Real Analysis and AI Policy. Extracurricularly, I was the President of Harvard Tech for Social Good, overseeing the end-to-end creation of technical projects for high-impact non-profits, as well as a research assistant for Kosuke Imai's ALARM project, which focused on quantitative gerrymandering research. Professionally, I worked as a full-stack developer intern at IBM over my sophomore and junior summers.

I am also a huge fan of comedy — specifically comedy writing. In my time at school, I was a staff writer at The Harvard Lampoon, the oldest graduate comedy organization in the country, as well as my original musical having been chosen by the Hasty Pudding Theatricals, the oldest theater organization in the country, to be their 177th annual show. The show had a 5-week residency in Cambridge, and proceeded to tour in New York City and Bermuda. Currently, I'm developing a parody novel to be published by Penguin Random House, and my work was just featured in the September issue of The Hollywood Reporter (see here).

Overall, I'm a generalist with a multidisciplinary background and multidisciplinary interests, having either studied or participated at a high level in the hard sciences, the social sciences, and the humanities. I excel at working with people and have a lot of experience working collaboratively on projects of varying complexity and on a deadline. I credit this most to the fact that I am a deeply passionate person and fast learner — if I care about something, I go all in every time.
