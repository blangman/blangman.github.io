01 — About

I work across the stack, usually where a clear technical problem meets people who need the result. At IBM I designed and shipped API Import, a framework that lets Guardium customers bring in external classification, vulnerability, and data-source definitions without waiting on a product release; the summer before, I built a connector that cut a three-month data-mapping process down to about a week. As Executive Director of Harvard Tech for Social Good I led 11 directors and roughly 100 members shipping software for partners like UNICEF, the CDC, and the Trevor Project — owning each project from stakeholder discovery through deployment.

The other half of my time goes to writing. I wrote Salooney Tunes, the 177th annual musical for Hasty Pudding Theatricals, and I'm on staff at The Harvard Lampoon. I care about careful language, whether it's a function name or a punchline, and about building things that are honest about what they do. Outside of that: number theory, AI ethics, backpacking, and arguing about commas in three languages.
