Projects

A few things I've built, and what they were for.

IBM2025

API Import

A flexible API integration framework for IBM Guardium. It lets customers import external classification, vulnerability, and data-source definitions on their own schedule, instead of waiting for those definitions to ship in a product release. Built to be extended; slated for the next major release.

IBM2024

Generic Universal Connector plugin

A standalone plugin for the Guardium Data Protection team that automated audit-log ingestion. It replaced a hand-built mapping process that took about three months with one that takes about a week.

ALARM Project2023–2024R

redist

An open-source R package for redistricting simulation and analysis, developed with Professor Kosuke Imai. I extended the package and used it to study Boston city-council districting plans with Markov chain and Sequential Monte Carlo methods.

Harvard Techfor Social Good2022–2025

Software for nonprofits

As Executive Director I owned the full lifecycle of 15 projects for partners including UNICEF, the CDC, the USDA, and the Trevor Project, from stakeholder discovery through deployment, and I designed and maintain the organization's website.
