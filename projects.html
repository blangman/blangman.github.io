Projects

A few things I've built, and what they were for.

Guardium tooling

IBM. 2024–2025.

Two pieces of IBM Guardium's data-protection product: a flexible API integration framework that lets customers import external classification, vulnerability, and data-source definitions on their own schedule instead of waiting for a product release (built to be extended, slated for the next major release), and a standalone plugin that automated audit-log ingestion for the Universal Connector, replacing a hand-built mapping process that took about three months with one that takes about a week.

Software for nonprofits

Harvard Tech for Social Good. 2022–2025.

As President I oversaw the end-to-end creation of 15 projects for partners including UNICEF, the CDC, the USDA, and the Trevor Project, from stakeholder discovery through deployment, and I designed and maintain the organization's website.

Snake

JavaScript. 2026.

A canvas Snake game with a fixed game-tick decoupled from the render loop, so speed stays frame-rate independent; eating food shortens the tick, so it gets faster as you score, and walls can wrap instead of killing you. See the code here.
